ABOUT US

Beth Medrash Ohel Michael was built around a belief that Torah should not live on the sidelines of a busy life — it should be at its center. What began as a place to create more opportunities for working men to learn has grown into a true hub of Torah, tefillah, friendship, and community. Every day, men with full schedules and real responsibilities make the choice to carve out time for something greater — to sit down with a chavruta, join a chabura, come before work, stay after a long day, and make Torah a permanent part of who they are.

But what makes Beth Medrash Ohel Michael special extends far beyond the seder. It is the energy of a vibrant Shabbat kiddush, the feeling of davening with purpose and kavanah, the warmth of families coming together for our holiday and community events, the sight of fathers and sons learning side by side at Avot U’Banim, and the countless moments throughout the year that turn a Beth Medrash into a true community. From the first seder at 5:30 in the morning until the final learning of the night, there is a spirit here that is difficult to describe but easy to feel. Our goal is not simply to add more hours of learning, but to help build lives shaped by Torah — stronger homes, deeper friendships, meaningful tefillah, genuine connection, and baalei batim who proudly see themselves as Bnei Torah.
