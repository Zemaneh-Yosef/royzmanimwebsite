CesiumJS 1.147.0 (https://github.com/CesiumGS/cesium), Apache-2.0 (LICENSE.md): cesium.js is a minified ESM bundle of only the exports horizon-3d.js uses (built with tsdown); cesium.d.ts re-exports their types from cesium-ambient.d.ts (Cesium's own Source/Cesium.d.ts). Workers, ThirdParty, Widgets/CesiumWidget and the Assets the 3D horizon page uses are from Build/Cesium as published on npm. Cesium's default world imagery (NaturalEarthII), the maki pin icons and the other widgets are left out.
